Hele-On Bus Rider Notes

Hawaiʻi County Hele-On Transit:

  • All Hele-On scheduled bus routes are currently fare-free!
  • Connecting Transfers: Guaranteed timed transfers occur at Keaʻau Old Volcano Road and Moʻoheau Bus Terminal.
  • Arrive at the stop at least 5 minutes prior to scheduled departure.
  • Bicycles can be transported on front-mounted bus racks.
