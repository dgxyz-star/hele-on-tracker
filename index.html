Hele-On GTFS Viewer - User Guide

Map Zooming & Gestures

👆 Double-Tap & Drag Zoom: Double-tap with your finger and hold on the second tap, then slide down to zoom in or slide up to zoom out of the map smoothly.

📍 Long-Press Map Location: Long-press anywhere on the map to automatically find and display the closest route and its nearby stops relative to that location.

Stop Markers & Directional Arrows: Bus stops are represented on the map as directional arrowhead icons. The tip of each arrow points in the exact direction the bus is traveling at that specific stop, helping you easily identify which side of the street or travel direction you are viewing. Tap on any stop arrow to open its pop-up window and view the full text list of stops and times for that route.

🔄 Multi-Route Stop Cycling: When a bus stop is shared by multiple active routes, tapping the stop arrow marker repeatedly cycles through each route's schedule card, updating the directional arrow and color to match the currently viewed route and looping back to the first route after the last.

Top Control Bar & Floating Menu

🔲 QR Code Button: Opens a share modal displaying a QR code and URL link to easily share this live app with others.

📝 Notepad Icon: Opens this full-window guide and functionality overview.

🎯 GPS Button: Centers and focuses the map on your current GPS location.

∞ Infinity Route Exploration Button: Enters an exploration mode displaying all available transit routes on the map. Tap individual routes to select them; once you tap any blank part of the map (not containing a route), your choices are saved, adding the selected routes to the drop-down menu and removing any unselected ones.

🚏 Nearest Route Button: Finds the closest route and stop relative to your current location.

➕ Add Route Button: Adds an extra route selection dropdown so you can view and compare multiple bus routes on the map simultaneously.

📅 Calendar Icon: Tap to pick a specific schedule date.

▲ Toggle Header Button: Expands or collapses the top header panel to maximize map viewing space.

🔄 Refresh Button: Located at the top of this user guide, tap to quickly reload and refresh schedule data—especially useful when running in borderless full-screen or home screen shortcut mode.

📲 Home Screen Shortcut (PWA): Add this app to your home screen from your browser's menu to launch it in a seamless, borderless standalone window.

On-Demand Route Panels & Trip Planner

When you select a route from the top dropdown, it displays cleanly on the map without cluttering your screen with extra panels.

  • Setting Start/End Stops: Tap any bus stop marker on the map and click the 📍 (Start) or 🏁 (End) buttons. Doing so automatically opens that route's bottom trip planner card.
  • 🔀 Automatic Transfer / Layover Summary Bar: When you set up multiple sequential routes that connect at a common transfer stop, a compact summary bar is automatically inserted directly between the route cards. It displays the shared transfer stop name, Route 1 arrival time, Route 2 departure time, and calculated layover/wait duration. You can browse through connection pairs with the ◀ ▶ buttons or tap specific trips.
  • 🔍 Find Sequential Closest Routes: Tap the magnifying glass icon inside any stop marker's popup to display routes serving that stop. When you press it multiple times, it will initially display the next closest route to that stop and then sequential closest routes.
  • Dual Selection Methods: Once a route card is open, you can either continue using the map popups or use the dropdown selectors inside the card to fine-tune your start and end stops.
  • Dynamic Equal-Share Space & Scrolling: Multiple active route cards automatically resize to share available vertical screen space equally. Each card is independently scrollable so you can easily thumb through all matching scheduled trips.
  • Dynamic Time-Based Status: Scheduled departure and arrival times inside route planner cards are dynamically color-coded based on the current local time: upcoming trips appear in green, while past or missed trips appear in red.
  • Clearing & Collapsing: Use the "Clear" button on the card to reset your selections and hide the panel, or tap the card header to collapse/expand it.
  • Bi-Directional Multi-Leg Propagation: Selecting any trip on an upstream, middle, or downstream route automatically synchronizes and updates the entire journey timeline in both directions across 3 or more routes. Downstream trips offer full flexibility for extended layovers, while preceding legs adjust backward to guarantee on-time connecting arrivals.
